# Marketing Plan — complyAI

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Indie fintech founders and early-stage fintech teams (1-20 people) who need compliance but can't afford enterprise solutions.

**Secondary:** Side-project fintech builders, Stripe Atlas founders, YC fintech grads, and solo developers launching financial products.

**Tertiary:** AI startup founders adding fintech features (payments, lending, wallets) who need regulatory guidance.

## Positioning

### Tagline Options
- *"Compliance for indie fintechs — no sales calls, no $500/mo minimums."*
- *"The RegTech you can actually afford."*
- *"Your first compliance officer. $49/mo."*

### Key Messaging Pillars

1. **Affordable** — Starting at $49/mo vs $500+/mo for enterprise alternatives
2. **Self-service** — No demos, no sales calls, no procurement. Sign up, pay, get your checklists.
3. **Practical** — Generates real documents, not just references. ToS, Privacy Policies, Reg E disclosures.
4. **Built for you** — Understands indie fintechs because we're one too.

## Distribution Channels

### 1. Indie Fintech Communities (Highest ROI)

| Community | Strategy |
|-----------|----------|
| **Indie Hackers** | Write "how I built a compliance checklist generator" build logs. Share revenue numbers. Engage in fintech threads. |
| **Hacker News** | Launch post focused on price difference: "I built a RegTech for $49/mo because $500/mo is absurd." |
| **r/fintech, r/startups** | Value posts: "What regulations apply to your fintech? Here's a free checklist." |
| **fintech Discord servers** | Be present and helpful. Offer free checklists for community members. |
| **YC fintech Slack** | Share with YC fintech alums. Target companies that launched via Stripe Atlas. |

### 2. Content Marketing

**"Reg Change of the Week" Newsletter**
- Weekly email: "Here's what changed in fintech regulation this week"
- Free — builds audience and demonstrates expertise
- Includes "What This Means For Your Fintech" section
- Send via Customer.io or ConvertKit

**Blog Posts** (SEO-optimized, long-tail keywords)
- "Fintech Compliance Checklist 2026: What You Need to Know"
- "Do I Need a Money Transmitter License? A Guide for Indie Fintechs"
- "BSA/AML Requirements for Small Fintechs (You Don't Need a Full Compliance Dept)"
- "How to Write a Privacy Policy for Your Fintech App"
- "Regulation E vs Regulation Z: What's the Difference?"

**Pillar Page:** "The Complete Guide to Fintech Compliance" — authoritative, linkable.

### 3. Partnerships

| Partner | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| **Stripe Atlas** | Become a recommended compliance partner for Atlas-incorporated companies. Offer discount for Atlas founders. |
| **Mercury** | Integration: "Connect complyAI to your Mercury account." Cross-promote to their 100k+ startup customers. |
| **Airwallex** | Similar play — embedded finance platforms need compliance tools. |
| **Fintech newsletters** | Paid sponsorships in Fintech Business Weekly, The Diff, Fintech Nexus. |

### 4. Product-Led Growth

**Free Tier:**
- 1 compliance checklist (any fintech type)
- 1 document template generation
- No credit card required

**Viral Loops:**
- "Share this checklist with your co-founder" — social sharing built into checklist output
- "Is your fintech compliant? Test here for free" — shareable quiz/audit tool
- Referral program: 1 month free for each referral

### 5. Community-Led Growth

- **Free compliance audit** — post in communities offering to audit a fintech's compliance readiness
- **AMA sessions** — "Ask me about fintech compliance" (position founder as accessible expert)
- **Open-source the basic checklist generator** — GitHub repo with free checks → upsell to full product

## Launch Strategy

### Pre-launch (2 weeks before)
1. Start "Reg Change of the Week" newsletter (build email list)
2. Publish 3 blog posts targeting SEO keywords
3. Join 5 fintech communities and start being helpful
4. Build free "Fintech Compliance Audit" quiz (lead magnet)

### Launch Day
1. Post on Hacker News, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt
2. Email list with launch announcement + early-bird pricing ($29/mo for first 100 customers)
3. Post in all target communities
4. Offer free month with code INDIE25

### Post-launch (first 30 days)
1. Track which channels drive signups (UTM parameters everywhere)
2. Publish 1 blog post per week
3. Engage with every user who signs up — ask what they need
4. Build features based on real feedback
5. Apply to Stripe Atlas integration directory
