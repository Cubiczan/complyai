FAQ

Is complyAI a replacement for a lawyer? ▼

No. complyAI is a tool to help you understand what regulations apply, generate document templates, and monitor changes. It is not legal advice. We strongly recommend reviewing outputs with a qualified attorney.

Which fintech types do you support? ▼

Payments, lending, crypto/digital assets, P2P, neobanking, wealth/investment, and insurtech. We're adding more based on user demand.

What states do you cover? ▼

Currently: New York (DFS), California (DFPI), and Texas (Dept of Banking). We're expanding to all 50 states. The Growth plan includes multi-jurisdiction support.

Can I cancel anytime? ▼

Yes. No contracts, no commitments. Cancel from your dashboard, and your subscription ends at the billing period.

How is this different from ComplyAdvantage? ▼

complyAI is built for indie fintechs. We're self-service, 90% cheaper, and generate real documents (ToS, Privacy Policies, disclosures) — not just checklists. No sales calls, no enterprise onboarding.
